Rhythm note recorder

1. Choose the same clip you put in the audio folder. 2. Press Record and tap the three pads (keys A, S, D) in time with the song. 3. Press Preview to check it, fix single numbers in the box if needed. 4. Copy the list and paste it between the brackets of notes: [ ... ] of that song in script.js (RHYTHM_SONGS). Do one song at a time.
